Need to know

Good to
know.

Have a question before you order? Here are answers to some of the most common questions. Our team is ready to help with anything else.

Standard production is generally 5–7 business days. Rush production may be available for an additional fee. Contact the team to confirm the current timeline for your order.
Stock chain graphics are applied as vinyl over the metal rather than etched directly into the stock piece. Contact the team if you need help choosing the right option.
After artwork approval and receipt of the deposit, fully custom premier chains normally take around 3–4 weeks. Confirm the current production schedule with the team.
Custom pricing depends on the design and selected options. The website's current FAQ indicates a typical range of $450–$1,300. Request a current price guide before ordering.
Shipping varies by destination and carrier. Championship Chains is based in Ohio and offers insured shipping; contact the team for a current domestic or international quote.
